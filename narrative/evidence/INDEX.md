# Evidence in this site

This directory is the audit trail behind the narratives. The same files are in
the repository and on the Netlify site.

## Field notes from Insight Out

- `why1-agents-as-answerers/`: first 88 agent-answer runs, bank, scoring and
  collector;
- `why2-repeatability/`: repeat runs behind the source lottery;
- `why3-hard-sources/`: hard PDF, portal and archive questions;
- `why4-prodding/`: pressure-turn runs;
- `why5-measure-what-where/`: measurement, coordinate and satellite questions;
- `why6-our-stack/`: the same questions through our stack;
- `why7-expertise/`: expert-phrased questions and their naive twins.

Each benchmark keeps its design or asset note, code, result, scoring material
and per-model runs where available. `index.html` is the browser view.

## Form data

`paperroast/` contains the copied evidence cut for both Form data narratives:
findings, core and evaluation code, result files, the XLSForm census, cell
benchmark crops and answer manifest, and scorer tests. Its README says which
large or private inputs were not copied.

Evidence snapshots are not edited to make the prose look right. A changed
benchmark gets a fresh copied cut and a reviewed diff.
